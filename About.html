Cozy Furniture

Cozy Furniture is Sri Lanka's largest furniture manufacturers offering a diverse range of modern and traditional furniture. Since Cozy Furniture was established in 1986 it has achieved a rapid success and has expanded to over 235 showrooms globally, which now having 167 showrooms island wide. Ceylon Furniture’s success has grown from it’s ability to produce high quality furniture with outstanding customer service.

OUR VISION

We have a vision to be the best in providing total solutions in furniture and interiors to our clientele, with an aim to enhance their own perceptions on functional and innovative design.

OUR MISSION

We are on a mission to recognize our clients’ needs and wants and fulfill them with dedication and hard work. We strive to enrich our business by building a strong relationship with our clients.
